POET is a youth charity. The site carries their line: tuổi trẻ, nhiệt huyết, nghệ thuật. Young people show up at schoolhouses, spend time with the children, and keep the stories.

The public site is static pages on GitHub Pages: home, the team, corporate Đồng hành packages, and an individual Ủng hộ page. Donations are created in the browser and confirmed by a PayOS webhook on a Cloudflare Worker. A gift appears on the public wall only after an admin publishes it.
